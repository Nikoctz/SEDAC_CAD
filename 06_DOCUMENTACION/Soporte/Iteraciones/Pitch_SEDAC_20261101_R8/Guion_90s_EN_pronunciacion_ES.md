# SEDAC — 90-second pitch script

**Read the bold English lines aloud. The Spanish line is a pronunciation aid, not a translation.**

Estimated time: about 80–90 seconds with short pauses. This is a speaking estimate; practice with a timer.

## Slide 1 · 7 seconds

**A heavy pump. A steep hill. A forest fire.**

É jevi pamp. É stiip jil. É fórest fáier.

## Slide 2 · 9 seconds

**We ask how the pump is carried. We want to study support for the shoulders and hips.**

Ui ask jao de pamp is kárid. Ui uant tu stádi sapórt for de shóulders and hips.

## Slide 3 · 7 seconds

**This is the real pump we use as a reference. We still need to measure it.**

Dis is de ríal pamp ui iús as a réfrens. Ui stil niid tu mé-zher it.

## Slide 4 · 8 seconds

**P2.04 is our current CAD pre-design. It shows a frame and belt support.**

Pii tú point zírou fór is áuer kárent kad prí-disáin. It shóus a fréim and belt sapórt.

## Slide 5 · 4 seconds

**Without SEDAC, the pump uses rubber shoulder straps.**

Uizáut SÉ-dak, de pamp iúsis ráber shóulder straps.

## Slide 6 · 8 seconds

**With SEDAC, we add a frame and belt support. The fit still needs testing.**

Uiz SÉ-dak, ui ad a fréim and belt sapórt. De fit stil niids tésting.

## Slide 7 · 7 seconds

**P2.04 is still a pre-design. It has not been physically tested.**

Pii tú point zírou fór is stil a prí-disáin. It jas not bin físikali téstid.

## Slide 8 · 15 seconds

**This week, we plan to measure movement and orientation with IMUs in the lab. IMUs do not measure force. Our future goal is sensor alerts for possible risks. The alert system still needs testing.**

Dis uík, ui plan tu mé-zher múuvment and ori-en-téi-shon uiz ai-em-iús in de lab. Ai-em-iús du not mé-zher fors. Áuer fiúcher gól is sén-sor alérts for pósibl risks. Di alért sístem stil niids tésting.

## Slide 9 · 8 seconds

**Next, we will measure, review the data, improve, build, and test.**

Nekst, ui uil mé-zher, riviú de déita, imprúuv, bild, and test.

## Slide 10 · 7 seconds

**Same pump. New way to carry it. We are looking for feedback and support.**

Séim pamp. Niu uéi tu kéri it. Ui ar lúking for fíidbak and sapórt.
