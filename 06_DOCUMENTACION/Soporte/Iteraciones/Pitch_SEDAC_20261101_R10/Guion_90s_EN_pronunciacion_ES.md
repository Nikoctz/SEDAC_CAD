# SEDAC — 90-second pitch script · R10

**Read the bold English lines aloud. The Spanish line is a pronunciation aid, not a translation.**

Estimated time: about 85–90 seconds with short pauses. This is a speaking estimate; practice with a timer.

## Slide 1 · 5 seconds

**A heavy pump. A steep hill. A forest fire.**

É jevi pamp. É stiip jil. É fórest fáier.

## Slide 2 · 7 seconds

**We ask how the pump is carried. We want to study support for the shoulders and hips.**

Ui ask jao de pamp is kárid. Ui uant tu stádi sapórt for de shóulders and hips.

## Slide 3 · 5 seconds

**This is the real pump we use as a reference. We still need to measure it.**

Dis is de ríal pamp ui iús as a réfrens. Ui stil niid tu mé-zher it.

## Slide 4 · 7 seconds

**P2.04 is our current CAD pre-design. It shows a frame and belt support.**

Pii tú point zírou fór is áuer kárent kad prí-disáin. It shóus a fréim and belt sapórt.

## Slide 5 · 4 seconds

**Without SEDAC, the pump uses rubber shoulder straps.**

Uizáut SÉ-dak, de pamp iúsis ráber shóulder straps.

## Slide 6 · 7 seconds

**With SEDAC, we add a frame and belt support. The fit still needs testing.**

Uiz SÉ-dak, ui ad a fréim and belt sapórt. De fit stil niids tésting.

## Slide 7 · 6 seconds

**P2.04 is still a pre-design. It has not been physically tested.**

Pii tú point zírou fór is stil a prí-disáin. It jas not bin físikali téstid.

## Slide 8 · 20 seconds

**This week, we plan to compare the pump alone with the same pump plus SEDAC. We will keep the load, task, and conditions the same. We will agree on the setup with the professor. IMUs track movement, orientation, and sway, not force. Results are pending. Future alerts still need testing.**

Dis uík, ui plan tu kom-pér de pamp a-lóun uiz de séim pamp plas SÉ-dak. Ui uil kiip de lód, task, and kondíshons de séim. Ui uil a-grí on de sé-tap uiz de pro-fé-sor. Ai-em-iús track múuvment, ori-en-téi-shon, and suéi, not fors. Ri-sálts ar pénding. Fiúcher alérts stil niid tésting.

## Slide 9 · 12 seconds

**These are possible locations, not final choices. The IMU tracks movement and orientation. The probe reads local surface temperature near the belt. We must confirm both with the professor.**

Diiz ar pósibl lou-kéi-shons, not fáinal chóisis. Di ai-em-iú tracks múuvment and ori-en-téi-shon. De próub riids lóukal sérfis témpricher níar de belt. Ui mast kon-férm bóuz uiz de pro-fé-sor.

## Slide 10 · 6 seconds

**Next, we will measure, review the data, improve, build, and test.**

Nekst, ui uil mé-zher, riviú de déita, imprúuv, bild, and test.

## Slide 11 · 5 seconds

**Same pump. New way to carry it. We are looking for feedback and support.**

Séim pamp. Niu uéi tu kéri it. Ui ar lúking for fíidbak and sapórt.
